<Section id="overview" title="Overview">
  <Prose>
    A personal finance agent for people whose money is scattered across
    checking accounts, cards, brokerages, and a dozen subscriptions. It
    watches spending, catches waste, ties it back to savings and investment
    goals, and checks in on its own schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

    The design question is the interesting one: a dashboard shows you data
    and stops. An agent has to take a position — say what changed, say what
    it thinks you should do, and be legible enough that you can disagree
    with it.
