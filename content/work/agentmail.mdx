<Section id="decisions" title="Decisions.">
  <Prose>
    **The toggle switches vantage points, not features.** Three tabs for one
    event, so the demo argues the thing works end to end rather than listing
    what it can do.

    **The recipient's view has no product in it.** The client tab is a plain
    Gmail thread. Putting AgentMail chrome on that screen would contradict the
    promise, which is that the mail arrives as ordinary mail.

    **The SDK rail never leaves.** Code sits beside every state, scrolling
    against the demo, so a developer evaluating this never loses sight of what
    they would actually be writing.

    **The manager's nudge starts the clock.** A pinned message asking for
    fifty sales by end of day gives the demo a reason to be running. Without
    it, an inbox full of sent mail is just an inbox.
  </Prose>
</Section>

<Section id="next" title="Unresolved.">
  <Prose>
    - The three states do not animate between each other. They should read as
      one continuous scene viewed from three angles.
    - No mobile layout. The SDK rail and the inbox panel cannot sit side by
      side on a narrow screen.
    - The email copy is written to look plausible rather than tested against
      anything a salesperson would actually send.
  </Prose>
</Section>
